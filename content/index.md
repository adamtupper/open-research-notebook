AdORN stands for **Ad**am's **O**pen **R**esearch **N**otebook. I'm sharing my work here in real time so others can follow along, learn from what works (and what doesn’t), and maybe even collaborate. It’s all about being transparent, avoiding duplicated effort, and making science a bit more open and connected.

Curious about open notebooks? [Learn more here.](https://book.the-turing-way.org/reproducible-research/open/open-notebooks/)
